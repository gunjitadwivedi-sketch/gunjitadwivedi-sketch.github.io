Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Find quick answers to common questions about this website, the course material, and my projects. This page uses the **Bootstrap Accordion** component for a clean, navigable design, crucial for mobile usability.

The assignment focused on **performance optimization** (specifically fixing the LCP issue found in the Lighthouse audit), improving **responsiveness** with media queries, and ensuring high **accessibility** and **SEO** scores across all six pages of the website.

The low score was primarily due to an issue with **Largest Contentful Paint (LCP)** caused by a large, unoptimized image. The fix involved **compressing the image file** to reduce its byte size and adding the `max-width: 100%;` CSS property for efficient scaling.

All pages now include a **custom favicon** for branding, a consistent **footer**, updated **SEO meta tags**, and a fully working, responsive **navigation bar** that includes links to all six pages.
